Privacy Policy

Medtrack — Medicine Tracker

Effective Date: November 2026 • Version 1.0.0

Core Privacy Guarantee: Medtrack is built with a local-first architecture. Your medication schedules, prescription dosages, adherence history, and emergency medical records are stored directly on your personal device inside a sandboxed SQLite database. We do not sell, rent, or monetize your health data.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Medtrack ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, protects, and handles your information when you use our Android mobile application. We respect your medical privacy and are committed to maintaining the highest security standards for your personal health records.

2. Information We Collect and Store

Medtrack is designed to operate completely offline without requiring an account. Depending on how you use the app, the following data may be stored:

  • Medication & Prescription Data: Medicine names, delivery forms (tablets, injections, inhalers, etc.), dosages, scheduled intake times, inventory pill counts, and refill thresholds.
  • Adherence & Health Observations: Timestamps when doses are taken, snoozed, or skipped, along with optional logged side effects and physiological vitals (blood pressure, glucose, heart rate).
  • Emergency Medical ID: User-provided medical profile data including blood type, allergies, emergency contacts, organ donor status, and primary physician contact information.
  • Account Information (Optional): If you voluntarily sign in with Google or Firebase Authentication to enable cloud backup across devices, your Google display name, email address, and unique user identifier (UID) are used solely to associate your encrypted backup with your account.

3. Where Your Data is Stored

  • Default (On-Device Local Storage): When using Medtrack as a guest or without signing in, 100% of your data remains on your physical device in an isolated SQLite database. No data is transmitted to external servers.
  • Optional Cloud Backup (Google Cloud Firestore): If and only if you choose to sign in to an account, your medication records and adherence history are synchronized with Google Cloud Firestore over an encrypted TLS connection. This data is isolated using strict security rules that ensure only your authenticated account can read or modify your records.

4. Device Permissions & Why They Are Needed

Medtrack requests only the minimal permissions essential for core utility:

  • Exact Alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM / USE_EXACT_ALARM): Required to deliver life-critical pill reminder alarms at your scheduled dose times, even when the device is locked or in Doze mode.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Required to show dose reminders and medication adherence prompts.
  • Biometrics (USE_BIOMETRIC): Optional local fingerprint or Face Unlock to protect your medical records from unauthorized access. Biometric data is processed exclusively by your device's hardware secure enclave; Medtrack never accesses or stores biometric signatures.
  • Approximate Location (Optional): Used locally to determine ambient daylight and temperature for context-aware dashboard themes. Location coordinates are never logged, tracked, or sent to any advertising network.

5. Third-Party Services & Advertising

  • Zero Advertising: Medtrack contains no advertisements, commercial trackers, or third-party marketing SDKs.
  • No Data Brokers: We never share, sell, lease, or distribute your personal or medical data to third-party data brokers, insurers, or pharmaceutical firms.
  • Firebase Services (Google Cloud): Used exclusively for secure user authentication and cloud synchronization if you elect to sign in.

6. User Control & Data Deletion

You maintain complete ownership and control over your health records:

  • Instant Local Wipe: In the app, navigate to Menu → Settings → Security & Data Privacy → Clear All Local Data to permanently erase your entire on-device database, local preferences, and active alarms.
  • Account & Cloud Deletion: If you created an account, tapping Delete Account & Cloud Data in the app instantly purges your Firestore cloud database and deletes your authentication record.
  • Web Deletion Request: If you no longer have access to the app, you can request full data deletion at our Account & Data Deletion Page.

7. Clinical & Medical Disclaimer

Medtrack is an adherence aid and digital organizational tool. It is not an FDA-cleared diagnostic device or a substitute for professional clinical medical advice, prescription diagnosis, or individualized treatment plans from licensed medical professionals.
