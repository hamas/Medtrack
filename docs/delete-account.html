Data Retention & Deletion

Account & Data Deletion Request

Medtrack — Medicine Tracker • Google Play Data Safety Compliance

At Medtrack, we believe you should always maintain full custody and ownership over your medical history and personal data. This page provides step-by-step instructions on how to delete your account, medication logs, and associated records.

1. If You Use Medtrack as a Guest (Default Mode)

If you have never signed in with a Google account or email, none of your health data is stored on remote servers. Everything resides strictly inside your phone's private local SQLite sandbox.

To erase your local data immediately:

  1. Open the Medtrack app on your device.
  2. Tap the Menu (drawer/tab) and select Settings.
  3. Tap Security & Data Privacy.
  4. Under Data & Storage, tap Clear All Local Data.
  5. Confirm the action. All medication records, dose logs, vitals, streaks, and scheduled alarms are permanently destroyed immediately.

Alternative: You can also clear app storage via your device's Android Settings → Apps → Medtrack → Storage → Clear Storage, or simply uninstall the app.

2. If You Signed In with an Account (Cloud Backup)

If you created an account to sync medications across devices, you can delete your account and all associated cloud data directly from within the app:

  1. Open Medtrack and ensure you are signed in.
  2. Navigate to Menu → Settings → Security & Data Privacy.
  3. Scroll down to the Danger Zone and tap Delete Account & Cloud Data.
  4. Confirm your decision. The app immediately purges:
    • Your user profile and medical emergency card.
    • All prescription records and dose schedules stored in Cloud Firestore.
    • All adherence logs, vitals, and side-effect histories.
    • Your Firebase Authentication credentials.
    • All local SQLite caches and active system alarm triggers.

3. Requesting Deletion via Email (Web Request)

If you have uninstalled the app or cannot access your device, you can submit a deletion request to our administrative team. We will permanently delete your authentication record and all associated Cloud Firestore documents within 7 business days.

To request account and data deletion:

Send an email from your registered account email address to:

support@medtrack.app

Subject: Account and Data Deletion Request

Please include:

  • Your registered email address.
  • A brief statement confirming you want all associated cloud data and authentication records permanently purged.

Once processed, you will receive a confirmation email verifying that all records have been completely and irrecoverably purged.

4. Data Retention Period

We do not retain deleted data. Once deleted (either through the in-app deletion button or upon fulfilling an email request), your data is permanently removed from our active database and cannot be recovered.
